About

Veteran-led. Texas-based. Built to perform.

Izy Global Services LLC was founded in 2020 by a service-disabled veteran with one standard carried over from uniform: the mission gets done, and someone accountable owns it.

Today the company performs healthcare courier operations for one of Texas's largest public health systems, courier services for the Department of Veterans Affairs in Tennessee, and data-platform quality assurance for the Defense Security Cooperation Agency — from headquarters in Houston and an operations base in San Antonio.

We stay deliberately lean at the top. When you call, you reach leadership. When something needs to change mid-contract, it changes — without a ticket queue.
